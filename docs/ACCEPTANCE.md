# TA Allocations — public acceptance criteria

Derived from elicitable stakeholder needs (Phase 1). Instructor private defects are **not** listed here.

## Roles & scope

1. HOD allocates TAs to courses; no TA self-service portal or task timesheets in graded MVP.
2. HR / contracts / payroll are out of system.
3. Passwords hashed; login never returns password material.

## Constraints

4. Hard block: a TA’s planned weekly hours across assignments must not exceed **20**.
5. Department budget hours must not go negative from new assignments.
6. Prior course experience is a **soft** preference (highlight), not a hard eligibility filter.

## Allocation

7. Manual assign records TA, course, weekly hours and is visible on the review board.
8. Auto-allocation supports interchangeable policies (at least experience-first vs least-loaded); each policy ranks candidates but must still respect hard cap/budget.
9. HOD can review assignments per course and remaining capacity per TA.
